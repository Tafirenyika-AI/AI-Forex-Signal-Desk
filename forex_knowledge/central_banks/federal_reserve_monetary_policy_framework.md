# Federal Reserve Monetary Policy Framework

## Core Purpose

The Federal Reserve's monetary policy comprises "actions and communications to promote maximum employment, stable prices, and moderate long-term interest rates" as mandated by Congress.

## Current Review Initiative

The Federal Reserve announced in November 2024 a periodic examination of its monetary policy strategy, tools, and communications framework used to pursue maximum employment and price stability objectives.

## Organizational Structure

The Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) serves as the primary policymaking body, with meetings scheduled throughout the year. The Committee maintains published longer-run goals and monetary policy strategy, along with communication policies for both committee members and staff.

## Policy Tools Available

The Federal Reserve employs multiple mechanisms including open market operations, the discount window, reserve requirements, interest on reserve balances, reverse repurchase agreements, term deposit facilities, central bank liquidity swaps, and foreign repo operations.

## Reporting and Documentation

The Fed publishes the Monetary Policy Report, Beige Book, and Federal Reserve Balance Sheet Developments report to communicate policy positions and economic assessments to the public and Congress.

## Leadership Initiatives

Five task forces examine areas central to monetary policy conduct, focusing on communications, balance sheet policy, data sources, productivity and employment, and inflation frameworks.
